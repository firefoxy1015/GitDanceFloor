About us

Pneumatic experts since 2005

Hebei Shenli Pneumatic Machinery Co., Ltd. was established in 2005 with a total investment of 60 million yuan. We employ more than 60 people and produce more than 20 categories of pneumatic mining equipment, sold across 30+ provinces in China and exported to South America, Africa, Asia-Pacific and beyond.

The plant integrates research & development, production, sales, after-sales service and parts supply — providing a complete one-stop quality assurance for every customer.

We have successfully passed ISO 9001 certification and obtained the China Light & Medium Products Quality Assurance Center "Double-guarantee" demonstration unit certificate, in addition to multiple patents transferred from Epiroc (Sweden) for next-generation rock drills.
